# Mindclade documentation legal and reliance policy

| Document control | Value |

## Purpose and legal effect

This policy applies to repository READMEs, architecture descriptions,
diagrams, examples, specifications, runbooks, plans, generated documentation,
and other explanatory material (collectively, "Documentation"). It identifies
how an authorized reader may rely on Documentation and how Documentation
relates to Mindclade's legal and operational controls.

Documentation records engineering intent, procedures, and evidence. Except for
the specific terms in `LICENSE`, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`, `SECURITY.md`, or another
document that expressly says otherwise, Documentation does not by itself
create a contract, offer, warranty, representation, service level, fiduciary
duty, or other legally binding commitment. It does not amend a controlling
written agreement.

## Authority and precedence

Applicable law and a controlling written agreement govern first. A
topic-specific root policy governs its subject: `LICENSE` for first-party
rights, `NOTICE` and component license files for third-party material,
`SECURITY.md` for vulnerability reporting, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` for project
conduct, `CONTRIBUTING.md` for submissions, and `SUPPORT.md` for support
routing. Raise a conflict through the legal or contract channel in the
applicable agreement before relying on the conflicting text.

Repository visibility, access, git history, review, approval, or publication
does not itself grant authority, waive a right, create a confidentiality duty,
or identify a person authorized to bind Mindclade, LLC. Only an authorized
company representative acting through the applicable approval process may make
a company commitment.

## Rights, confidentiality, and branding

Documentation grants no license, ownership, trademark, publicity,
endorsement, affiliation, or redistribution right. The repository `LICENSE`,
applicable third-party terms, and the controlling written agreement define
those rights. Repository visibility or a confidentiality label is not by
itself the source of a confidentiality obligation; such obligations arise
under applicable law or an agreement.

Do not put credentials, customer or patient information, personal data,
private model material, restricted biological content, privileged
communications, or other protected material in Documentation unless an
approved system and documented authorization specifically permit it.

## Technical, regulatory, and professional reliance

A design, scaffold, plan, example, test result, rendered manifest, or status
statement is not proof of deployment, production qualification, certification,
legal compliance, safety, availability, or fitness for a particular use. Those
claims require current evidence from the authoritative system and the approval
specified by the owning qualification or compliance process.

Documentation is not legal, medical, clinical, financial, or regulatory
advice. No Documentation may be used as the sole basis for diagnosis,
treatment, patient care, safety-critical activity, regulated submission, or a
decision affecting a person's legal rights. A claim that Mindclade complies
with a law, regulation, certification, or external framework must identify its
approved scope, evidence, owner, review date, and any controlling agreement.

## Privacy and security representations

Architecture and control descriptions state repository requirements; they do
not represent that every environment or product has deployed every described
control. These engineering repositories are not a consumer privacy notice,
product terms of service, data-processing agreement, or customer security
schedule. Customer-facing privacy, security, retention, residency, and service
commitments exist only in the applicable approved notice or written agreement.

Vulnerability research and reporting are governed by `SECURITY.md`. No other
Documentation expands testing scope, grants access to third-party systems or
data, promises a bounty, or binds a third party or government authority.

## Operational use

Commands and procedures do not grant access or authorization. Before acting,
the operator must verify the target, current state, required approval, blast
radius, rollback path, and authority under the controlling process. Statements
about live state, performance, recovery, or production readiness must be
validated against current connected evidence; repository text alone is not
proof of live state.

## Third-party material

Third-party software, data, models, media, fonts, standards, and other
independently licensed material retain their own rights, restrictions,
notices, attribution, consent, provenance, patent, and acceptable-use terms.
Those terms control for that material. See `NOTICE`, manifests, lockfiles,
SBOMs, provenance, and accompanying license texts.

## Protected rights

Nothing in Documentation prohibits or restricts a protected disclosure,
lawful report to a government authority, cooperation with an investigation,
or another non-waivable right. The protected-disclosure notice in `LICENSE`
applies to the Materials described there.

## Review and change control

Legal-effecting changes require review through the repository's approval path
and by qualified counsel when they concern licensing, contributor rights,
privacy promises, protected disclosures, confidentiality, trademarks,
regulated claims, or contractual commitments. No repository text represents
that counsel approved a document. Do not include sensitive material in a
public issue or an unapproved email; use the legal, contract, privacy,
security, or conduct channel designated for the matter.
